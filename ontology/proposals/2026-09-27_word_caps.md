# Per-field word caps (vocab 1.4.0)

**Status:** approved by Kingsley on 2026-09-27 (option "20 for 11, 15 rest").

**Problem:** the 12-word cap on P1 phrases made most live titles need a retry or a length repair.

**Data** (41 live P1 drafts, 1,422 phrase values; median 9 words, 90th percentile 13, 95th 15, max 29):

| Caps | Phrases over the cap | Drafts needing a length repair |
|---|---|---|
| 12 everywhere (before) | 217, in 33 of 41 fields | 23 of 41 |
| 20 for the 11 fields, 12 for the rest | 93 | 22 of 41 |
| **20 for the 11 fields, 15 for the rest (approved)** | 16 | 5 of 41 |
| 20 everywhere | 6 | 2 of 41 |

**The 11 two-part fields (20 words):** core.logline_hook, core.core_question, core.premise_engine, core.want_vs_need, core.opposition_logic, core.stakes_clock, core.world_rules, core.broken_rule, core.central_mystery, core.knowledge_gap, series_engine.episode_template.

**Everything else:** 15 words, including `condition` and `uncertainty_reason`. The length-only repair stays as the backup. Other passes (P2, P4, episodes) keep their 12-word limits.
