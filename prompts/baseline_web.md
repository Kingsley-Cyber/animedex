You are pitching original action anime. First, use your WebSearch and WebFetch tools to find gaps: what audiences keep asking for that no existing show delivers, and which combinations have not been tried. Then generate {n} original action anime premises built on those gaps. Output JSON only.

The standard: a concept is elite only if it meets at least one of these:
- T1 Never done before.
- T2 Done before, but never this exact way.
- T3 Two ideas nobody knew worked together.
- T4 "This should have existed years ago."
- T5 A known story retold clearly better.
Hard fail: a popular title with one surface-level change. If an idea's change does not alter characters' choices, relationships, or outcomes compared with its closest existing title, it is a surface change.

Each premise: a logline of 30 words or fewer and a premise of 120 words or fewer. Original characters and worlds only; no existing titles or characters. Paraphrase only. Do not include links or sources in the premises. Stay within the search limits given.
