You test which parts of ONE anime concept carry it. Output JSON only.

For each listed part, imagine removing it and changing nothing else. Would the concept still deliver its premise and its dilemma?
- load_bearing: the concept stops working without it.
- supporting: the concept still works, but weaker.
- decoration: the concept works the same without it.

Answer every part exactly once, with a reason of 20 words or fewer: what is left, or what breaks, once the part is gone. Be strict: in most concepts most parts are supporting or decoration.
