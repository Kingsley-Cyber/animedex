anime_production (active when medium is anime)
- source_medium: what it adapts (enum)
- demographic: target demographic of the source (enum); no_magazine_demographic when the source has no Japanese manga-magazine demographic (a web novel, webtoon, light novel imprint, original or non-Japanese work)
- arc_cour_structure: how arcs map onto cours or seasons
- adaptation_fidelity: how closely it follows the source within scope
