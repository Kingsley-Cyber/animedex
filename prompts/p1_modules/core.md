core (always active)
- logline_hook: the one-line hook a viewer would repeat
- core_question: the thematic question the story keeps asking
- premise_engine: the situation that keeps generating story
- primary_feeling: the dominant feeling the title sells
- tone: the tonal register
- want_vs_need: the lead's surface want vs deeper need
- flaw: the lead's defining flaw (condition: when it shows)
- moral_line: what the lead will not do (condition: what would make them cross it)
- central_opposition: the main opposing force
- opposition_logic: why the opposition acts as it does
- stakes_clock: what is at stake and what time pressure exists
- world_rules: the world rule that shapes every conflict
- borrowed_template: the known system or genre template it borrows (null if none)
- broken_rule: the rule of that template it breaks (null if none)
- unserved_appetite: the audience desire it serves that similar works rarely do
- central_mystery: the main open question kept from audience or characters (null if none)
- knowledge_gap: who knows what the others do not
- reveal_cadence: how often and how large the reveals are
- outcome: reception label (enum; verified on the web later)
